# Journal Targeting Plan (Stage-1 Requirement)

## Goal
Suggest 5 journals and finalize publication priority while considering discoverability and cost-effectiveness.

## Priority Rules from Assignment
- Target mostly Q3 (cost-effective and easier reference sourcing).
- Include Q2 options as secondary priority.
- Ensure DOI-backed references; avoid no-DOI journals for this study.

## Proposed 5 Journals

| Priority | Journal | Quartile (Typical) | Scope fit | Cost/Access note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IEEE Access | Q2/Q1-like depending category | Applied ML + interdisciplinary implementation papers | APC model; widely indexed |
| 2 | Expert Systems with Applications (Elsevier) | Q1/Q2-like | Real-world ML decision systems | Often paid OA/closed options |
| 3 | Applied Soft Computing (Elsevier) | Q2 | Hybrid intelligent algorithms | Paid options; broad readership |
| 4 | Computers in Biology and Medicine (Elsevier) | Q2/Q3-like | Biomedical ML case studies | Cost varies by publication route |
| 5 | International Journal of Intelligent Systems and Applications in Engineering (or similar Scopus-indexed Q3 venue) | Q3 | Practical implementation studies | Typically more cost-effective |

## Finalized Publishing Order
1. IEEE Access
2. Applied Soft Computing
3. Computers in Biology and Medicine
4. Expert Systems with Applications
5. Scopus-indexed Q3 fallback journal

## Why this order?
- Balances visibility, feasibility, and topic fit.
- Keeps at least one cost-effective Q3 fallback while retaining stronger Q2 opportunities.
